# Decision rules

1. `ARCHIVED_ANALYTIC_IDENTITY`: explicit angular RHS proof and independent archived script test, on arbitrary states (not just Q550 orbit).
2. `CURRENT_ENGINE_FIELD_VERIFIED`: authenticated original structural_jet loaded, many off-trajectory states show charge derivative zero and anti-rotation equivariance to floating-point precision. This is a **numerical structural check**, complementing the analytic proof of the archived RHS.
3. `ENGINE_MATCHED_HISTORIC_ANGULAR_RHS`: compare original field angular components to independent archived source. Discrepancies or unavailable comparisons are reported, never silently ignored.
4. `NATIVE_TRACE_CONFIRMED`: full v18.52l 10D state traces reproduce constant C over all 20 series, with old and refined solutions.
5. `NOETHER_DALEMBERT_INTERPRETATION`: follows from invariant angular kinetic and potential, and absence of angular nonconservative force in the explicit RHS. Not equivalent to autonomous Hamiltonian conservation of the full driven system.
6. `GLOBAL_NONCOLLAPSE_CERTIFIED`: **NOT** unless a future energy bound or comparable control is separately established. Finite-horizon data cannot certify it.
7. `TOPOLOGICAL_CHARGE_CERTIFIED`: **NOT**. C is continuous, not a winding integer.

Stop on a SHA mismatch. Deliberately forced angular source must violate the identity. Different states/drive phases/κ values must be probed; trajectory-only preservation is insufficient.
