# Scientific Decision Rules

- A technical PASS means authenticated, reproducible, finite and internally consistent calculations. It does NOT mean that the proposed physical recovery mechanism was confirmed.
- A model-reconstructed lateral share exceeding 50% in the qualified interval is reported as **sampled lateral dominance** and not as dissipation, return or restoring acceleration.
- A permanent kappa0 difference can explain parameter sensitivity but cannot test recovery *after the perturbation is removed*, because the difference stays present in the governing equation.
- The finite initial-velocity-kick experiments keep kappa0 and the drive fixed and are an explicit, different intervention. Their instantaneous Cartesian position is conserved. Their reported state-distance contraction is a finite-horizon diagnostic in a declared coordinate scale.
- Compare fixed material tau, keep fixed-drive-xi differences available, and quantify how projection/tangent differences affect `delta a_perp`.
- A derived acceleration from sampled archived trajectories is an interpolation-based estimate. Agreement with the original nonlinear RHS and derivative refinement must be evaluated and reported.
- No result is a rigorous interval certificate or a general statement about all possible physically admissible forces, perturbations or whole-period dynamics.
