# v18.52b — Scientific decision rules

1. Numerical audit PASS is independent of physical-sign conclusions.
2. For each physical parameter value, source-channel supports remain the two historic velocity components (indices 5 and 7). The residual channel is retained.
3. Evaluate the signed margin **and** full turning rate over all qualified sections; do not infer the latter from the former when a residual channel is present.
4. The entire scan is numerical and discretely sampled in both `kappa0` and `xi`.
5. A passing outermost parameter sample is a **sampled extent**, not a mathematically certified robustness radius or proof that the continuum between samples is positive.
6. A sign loss reached by an authentic nonlinear path is a **candidate physical turning-budget boundary**; refine its bracket with new nonlinear paths.
7. Solver stiffness, integration failure, numerical disagreement or loss of source consistency are **unresolved numerical limits**, never evidence of a sign transition.
8. A near-zero margin without a documented error separation is classified unresolved.
9. Dependence of signed-channel contributions on the original model-coordinate source supports must remain explicit.
10. No material-return, geometric holonomy or constitutive memory theorem follows from this test.
