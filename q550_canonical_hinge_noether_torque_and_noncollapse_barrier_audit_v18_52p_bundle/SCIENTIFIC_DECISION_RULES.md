# Scientific decision rules — v18.52p

**Level 0 — canonical coordinate check:** same orthogonal planar `R` applied to physical `(Q,P)` yields `S^TΩS=Ω` and invariant *value* `J(Q',P')=J(Q,P)`. This is true independently of dynamics, and cannot certify a Noether charge.

**Level 1 — kinetic on-trajectory balance:** independently check `Jkin'≈q×a` and native polar identity `J1=r1²*w1`. A nonzero torque and genuine drift in `Jkin(tau)` falsifies its strict conservation, but does not rule out a corrected full-model charge involving drive/memory or a different canonical momentum.

**Level 2 — physical Noether certification:** requires an **explicit actual action/Hamiltonian**, native canonical coordinates and momenta, nonconservative source terms, and direct rotational equivariance / Poisson-bracket checks. NOT AVAILABLE in this package; report this status plainly. Do not label `Jkin` as a proven Noether invariant.

**Level 3 — finite-horizon angular obstruction:** a nonzero-signed `Jkin` together with bounded observed speed yields an a posteriori positive radius bound at *sampled times* and a positive kinematic angular-energy term `J²/(2r²)`. This may support the user's geometrical noncollapse intuition **over the measured trajectory**, but cannot establish all-time impossibility of collapse without a true conserved charge and globally justified speed/energy bound.

**Level 4 — global no-collapse theorem:** requires a rigorous continuous-time nonzero charge bound, justified effective kinetic radial inequality and suitable physical energy/speed control, with error bounds or exact hypotheses. NOT CLAIMED here.

**Topological qualification:** signed open-curve phase turns are not integer topological winding. A closed non-singular loop and stable homotopy class are required before declaring topological charge. No identification between Noether momentum and topological winding is presumed.

A report containing `Noether NOT CERTIFIED` is an appropriate rigorous audit result rather than a negative judgment on the original geometric observation.

**Log-polar coordinate caveat:** the native absolute radii `r_i=exp(ell_i)>0` for finite chart values. This positivity is a property of the chart and does not prove a uniform positive radius bound or exclude an asymptotic `ell_i -> -infinity` approach. It is distinct from the **relative** separation radius `||Qkick-Qreference||`, which may vanish even when both absolute radii are positive.
