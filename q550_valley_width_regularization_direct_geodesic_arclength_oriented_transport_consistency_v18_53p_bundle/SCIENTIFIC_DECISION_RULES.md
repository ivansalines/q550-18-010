2. All reported v18.53p numeric rows are **derived post-processing**, with `new_physical_ivps=0`. The 10 historical v18.53o physical integrations are not repeated.

4. An apparent derivative-integral anomaly is called **numerical** unless it survives grid refinement, global bounded optimization, knot-contact checks and interpolant sensitivity; even survival does not prove a material phenomenon.
