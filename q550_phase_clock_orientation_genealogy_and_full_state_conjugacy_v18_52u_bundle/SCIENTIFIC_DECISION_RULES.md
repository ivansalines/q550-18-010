# Scientific classification policy

- `AUTHENTIC_GENEALOGY_WITH_EXTERNAL_DRIVE_DISTINCT_FROM_SIMPLE_COORDINATE_RELABEL`: authenticated source and native traces; external Theta follows negative x_reference, engine follows positive x; archived `-Theta_ext` torque is supported while relabel `+Theta_ext` is not, and simple map fails full-field conjugacy.
- `AUTHENTIC_GENEALOGY_COMPATIBLE_WITH_PASSIVE_RELABEL`: source and traces with `+Theta_ext` force fit and explicit source mapping; requires all-field verification, not just 10D force fit. Always label any unsupported all-field conjugacy separately.
- `AUTHENTIC_GENEALOGY_SIGN_DISCORDANCE_NOT_YET_CLASSIFIED`: verified lineage but force sign comparison ambiguous or both fail.
- `ANALYTIC_CANDIDATE_MAP_TESTS_ONLY_PARENT_MISSING`: parent not found; report exact coordinate identity and explicit rejection of naive maps, never claim to have traced the external theta source.
- `AUTHENTIC_PARENT_BUT_NATIVE_TRACES_MISSING`: parent verified, source genealogy captured but native force discrimination pending.

- Never claim a topological invariant or global energy bound.
- Never silently assume a kick's x(t) defines its inherited external clock.
- Never use the 11th column of v18.52l as Theta: that column is material time.
- A negative result for the tested maps does not exclude every possible nonlinear conjugacy.
