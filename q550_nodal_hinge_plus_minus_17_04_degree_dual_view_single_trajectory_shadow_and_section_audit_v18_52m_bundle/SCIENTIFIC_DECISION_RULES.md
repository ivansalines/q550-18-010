# Scientific decisions

- Source SHA mismatch or physical `D` / `G` identity mismatch: reject the run.
- Missing physical Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4/Q5 data: reject the run.
- A +/- mirror relation that follows from the dual projection equations is an **algebraic observation only**.
- Same-time double shadows are **not two physical filaments**.
- Cross-view zero crossings are not recurrence or closure; inspect full 5D displacement and angular mismatch at their actual, generally distinct, material times.
- A visually preferred angle must survive fixed controls (0, 10, 25, 35 degrees), fixed hidden-axis substitutions (Q3/Q4/Q5), PCA holdout, and all eight Q550 trials. No post hoc choice is promoted to an invariant.
- A real tube/cone interpretation requires a specified 5D geometric object and a model-driven section transport law; radius-vs-axial fits alone are only preliminary.
- A topological invariant needs an explicitly defined coordinate-free quantity with a proof or numerical certificate of preservation. This audit defines none; it reports observer-dependent angular winding diagnostics only.
- A genuine material-moving gate model requires a separately defined, dynamically generated observer transport and cannot be inferred from static +/- tilts.

- Report within-section `D` and hinge-perpendicular-radius dispersion rather than assuming a section invariant. Compare same-view consecutive postures with both same and antipodal signs, preserving temporal order.
